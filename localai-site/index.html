01 / SERVICES

What a local AI system can do for you

Every module below runs on your hardware, inside your network. If your work is confidential by law, by contract, or by common sense, this is how you get modern AI without the disclosure problem.

MODULE 01 — RAG

Private document intelligence

“Which of our contracts auto-renew in the next 90 days?” Ask questions across every contract, case file, patient record, or project archive you have — in plain English — and get answers with citations back to the source page.

You get: an on-premise search and Q&A system indexed over your full document set, integrated with how you already store files.

The index and every query stay on your server. Built for legal, medical, and accounting work.

MODULE 02 — MAIL

Email triage & drafting

An assistant that reads your inbox, flags what actually needs you, summarizes the rest, and drafts replies in your voice — ready for a one-click review and send.

You get: a local assistant wired into your existing mail client or server, tuned on your own sent mail.

Your correspondence is processed on your machine. No inbox contents leave your network.

MODULE 03 — CONTENT

Marketing & content generation

Blog posts, social copy, listing descriptions, client newsletters — generated locally and grounded in your past content, so it sounds like you and not like a template.

You get: a content workstation trained on your brand voice, with reusable templates for your recurring formats.

Your unpublished drafts, strategy, and client names never touch a third-party model.

MODULE 04 — AUDIO

Meeting transcription & summaries

Record a client meeting, a deposition prep session, or a patient consult and get an accurate transcript, a summary, and a list of action items minutes later.

You get: local speech-to-text (Whisper-class) with automatic summaries, speaker labels, and action-item extraction.

No recording is ever uploaded. Essential for privileged, HIPAA-adjacent, or otherwise sensitive conversations.

MODULE 05 — SUPPORT

Customer support assistant

A chatbot that answers from your actual documentation, policies, and product data — for your website visitors or your internal staff — instead of improvising.

You get: a grounded assistant running on your server, with an admin view of every conversation and a clean escalation path to a human.

Customer conversations and your internal knowledge base stay on infrastructure you control.

MODULE 06 — DATA

Data analysis & reporting

“Show me revenue by client for Q1, and which invoices are more than 30 days late.” Natural-language queries over your spreadsheets, databases, and business records — plus recurring reports that write themselves.

You get: a local analysis layer over your existing data sources and scheduled, automated reporting.

Your financials are analyzed where they live. Nothing is exported to anyone’s cloud.

MODULE 07 — OPS

Workflow automation

Intake forms that file themselves, CRM records that update from emails, invoices that get read and routed, scheduling that handles itself. AI wired into the tools you already run.

You get: integrations between a local AI engine and your existing software, with the AI step kept fully on-device.

Automation without handing a third party a feed of your operations.

Custom builds

If it can be done with AI, it can be done locally. Vision systems, fine-tuned domain models, multi-site deployments, research tooling — if you have a problem that doesn’t fit a box above, that’s usually the interesting kind.

03 / WHY LOCAL

Local AI vs. cloud AI, factually

Cloud AI services are genuinely capable, and for plenty of businesses they’re the right call. But if your work involves privileged, regulated, or competitively sensitive information, the comparison looks like this:

Comparison of local on-premise AI and cloud AI services
Attribute Local / on-premise Cloud AI services
Where your data goes Nowhere. Inference, storage, and search happen inside your network. To the provider’s data centers, governed by their terms and retention policies.
Confidentiality & compliance Compatible with attorney–client privilege, HIPAA workflows, and CMMC/ITAR-adjacent environments, because no third party touches the data. Possible, but requires vendor agreements (BAAs, DPAs), audits, and ongoing trust in someone else’s controls.
Cost model One-time build, plus an optional support plan. No per-seat or per-token charges. Subscription and usage fees that continue forever and rise with adoption.
Internet dependency None. Works fully offline, including during outages. Unavailable when your connection — or the provider — is down.
Vendor lock-in None. You own the hardware, the models, and the data. Swap any part later. Models, prices, and terms can change under you with little notice.
Model behavior Pinned model versions. The system behaves the same tomorrow as today; you choose when to upgrade. Models are updated or deprecated on the provider’s schedule.
Raw capability Today’s open models handle the large majority of real business tasks well; the gap to frontier cloud models keeps narrowing. The very largest frontier models lead on the hardest reasoning tasks.

That last row is the honest one. If your workload truly needs the absolute frontier, I’ll tell you in the first call.

06 / FAQ

Questions people actually ask

How good are local models compared to ChatGPT?

Closer than most people expect. For the work this site describes — document Q&A, drafting, summarization, transcription, structured data tasks — today’s open models are excellent, and a system tuned to your documents and voice often beats a general cloud model at your specific job. The very largest cloud models still lead on the hardest open-ended reasoning. If your workload is in that category, I’ll tell you in the first call.

What hardware do I need? Do you supply it?

Either works. I can build and deliver a machine spec’d for your workload — from a quiet desktop for one person to a rack server for a whole practice — or deploy onto capable hardware you already own. A useful single-user system starts at roughly the cost of a high-end workstation; I’ll give you exact numbers in the proposal.

What does it cost?

It depends on scope, which is why every engagement starts with a free consultation and ends step one with a written, fixed quote. The structure is always the same: a one-time build cost (hardware plus my engineering), and an optional ongoing support plan. There are no per-user or per-token fees, ever.

Can it work fully offline?

Yes — that’s the point. Once deployed, the system needs no internet connection to run. Models, search indexes, and your data all live on your hardware. Some clients keep their AI server on an isolated network segment with no outbound route at all.

What about updates as models improve?

The field moves fast, and your system shouldn’t fossilize. Support plans include periodic model upgrades: I test new open models against your actual workload and roll them in when they’re a clear win. Because you own the hardware, an upgrade is a software change — not a new subscription tier.

Is my data really private?

Yes, verifiably. The system runs on your hardware inside your network, and you (or your IT contractor) can confirm with standard network monitoring that nothing leaves. I’ll happily walk your compliance or security reviewer through the architecture. Even this website makes zero external requests — there’s a live counter in the footer.

How long does setup take?

A single-workstation Starter setup is typically up within 1–2 weeks of the proposal being signed. Business deployments with document indexing and workflow integration usually run 3–6 weeks depending on scope. Custom work is quoted with its own timeline.
